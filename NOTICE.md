Third-party material used by this course, and what each licence obliges.

This file exists because gate A4 requires it, and A4 requires it because an

dependencies is making exactly the omission it asks reusers not to make.

## Synthetic voice

Audio in this course is synthesised with the Piper voice `en_GB-cori-medium`, trained from
scratch on **public-domain** LibriVox recordings, <https://librivox.org>. No attribution is
owed; the credit is given anyway.

## Fonts

Source Sans 3 and JetBrains Mono, both SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full
attribution and per-file provenance in `static/fonts/ATTRIBUTION.md`; licence
texts in [kit/design/fonts/LICENSES](https://github.com/boulingua/kit/tree/main/design/fonts/LICENSES).

## Framework

CEFR-aligned descriptors from [boulingua/curriculum](https://github.com/boulingua/curriculum),
which paraphrases the Council of Europe's Companion Volume rather than
reproducing it. No Council of Europe text is copied into this repository.
