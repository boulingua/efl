## Learning objectives

- *I can name 12 feelings.*
- *I can ask *How are you?* in five different ways.*
- *I can use *because* and *so* to give simple reasons.*

## Lead-in story

Lina is in a strange mood. Sam asks why. She says, "I don't know — I'm tired and slightly proud and a tiny bit jealous all at once." Sam nods. "That is called Tuesday afternoon," he says.

## 1. Activate {#activate}
**Mood scan.** Stand on a line: *very happy* (left wall) → *very sad* (right wall). Find your spot. No talking. The teacher reads three short statements; students adjust their position.

## 2. Input {#input}
### Vocabulary — feelings

*happy, sad, tired, excited, proud, nervous, scared, angry, jealous, calm, surprised, disappointed, embarrassed, grateful.*

### Grammar — *because / so*

*because* + reason: *I'm tired **because** I went to bed late.*
*so* + result: *I went to bed late, **so** I'm tired.*

### Asking *How are you?*

- *How are you?*
- *How are you doing?*
- *How's it going?*
- *How was your day?*
- *Are you OK?*

## 3. Practise {#practise}
### Niveau G

1. Match: happy — :), sad — :(, surprised — :O, tired — zzz. (T / F)
2. Fill in *because* or *so*: I'm hungry __________ I didn't eat breakfast. He's tired, __________ he is going to bed early.

### Niveau M

3. Build 4 sentences with *because* + 4 with *so* about your real day.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**G.** 1. all true. 2. because / so.

**M.** 3. Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce {#apply}
**Pair speaking — *Today's Mood*.** Two minutes each. Use at least three feelings + one *because* + one *so* sentence.

### Sample

*— I'm a bit nervous because we have a maths test.*
*— I get it. I am proud, though, so it balances out.*

## 5. Reflect {#reflect}
- [ ] I can name 12 feelings.
- [ ] I can ask *How are you?* in 5 ways.
- [ ] I can use *because* and *so* in short reasons.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence using something you learned in this Unit.*

## Common pitfalls

- *Because* + clause; *because of* + noun: *because the rain* → ✗ / *because of the rain* OR *because it was raining* → ✓.
- *so + because* in one sentence sounds redundant.
- Pastoral note: keep the mood-line low-stakes. Some feelings are private.
