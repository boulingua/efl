EFL terminology and BW-specific exam terms used across the site.
Each entry has a stable HTML anchor (e.g. `#klassenarbeit`) so
Units can deep-link on first mention.

## EFL / didactics

[**CEFR — Common European Framework of Reference for Languages.**]{#cefr}
The six-level (A1–C2) descriptor system. Every Unit's "Can-do"
objectives are written in CEFR style.

[**CLIL — Content and Language Integrated Learning.**]{#clil}
Teaching content (e.g. a topic in geography or social studies)
through English. This site is CLIL-inflected: meta-language is
English throughout, and many Units anchor language work to a
non-language content theme.

[**i+1.**]{#i-plus-1} Krashen's principle that input should sit
just above the learner's current level. The Niveau-tagging on
Units helps teachers match input to the right group.

[**Productive vs. receptive skills.**]{#productive-receptive}
Speaking and writing are productive (the learner produces
language). Listening and reading are receptive. Most Units
balance both within the five-step shape.

[**Scaffolding.**]{#scaffolding} Temporary supports — sentence
stems, vocabulary lists, picture prompts, partial outlines — that
let learners attempt tasks beyond their current independent
level.

[**Five-step template.**]{#five-step} Activate → Input → Practise
→ Produce → Reflect. The default Unit shape on this site. The
exam-prep variant is Task → Model → Strategy → Attempt →
Feedback.

## Exam terminology

[**curriculum framework ("Bildungsplan").**]{#bildungsplan}
The binding regional curriculum document on which this site is
oriented (see [References](/references/) for the specific
framework documents). Two are relevant here: *curriculum framework
("Bildungsplan") 2016 — Sekundarstufe I* (for Kl. 5–10) and
*curriculum framework ("Bildungsplan") gymnasiale Oberstufe 2021*
(for Kl. 11–13).

[**basic course ("Basisfach") / advanced course ("Leistungsfach").**]{#basisfach-leistungsfach}
Two intensity levels in the gymnasiale Oberstufe. Basisfach (3
hours per week) targets "basic course ("Basisfach")" school-leaving
examination ("Abitur") tasks; Leistungsfach (5 hours per week)
targets the more demanding "advanced course ("Leistungsfach")"
tasks. Track E Kl. 11–13 Units are tagged either *E-BF* or
*E-LF*.

[**grading grid (Bewertungsraster).**]{#bewertungsraster} The
grading grid (Inhalt + Sprache, with weighting) used to mark
school-leaving examination ("Abitur") productions. Every Track E
Kl. 11–13 Unit ships its own grading grid (Bewertungsraster).

[**expected-answer profile (Erwartungshorizont).**]{#erwartungshorizont}
The expected-answer profile that accompanies every school-leaving
examination ("Abitur") task. Per Unit, the expected-answer profile
(Erwartungshorizont) sits in a collapsed callout under the exam
example.

[**class test ("Klassenarbeit").**]{#klassenarbeit} The standard
written assessment in Sekundarstufe I, typically 45 or 90 minutes,
with kompetenzorientierte Aufgaben in BW format. Every Track G+M
Unit ends with a worked class test ("Klassenarbeit") at its Niveau.

[**Klausur (assessment).**]{#klausur} The Oberstufe equivalent of
a class test ("Klassenarbeit"). Typically 4 hours, 90 BE
(Comprehension + Analysis + Composition + Mediation). Every Track
E Kl. 11–13 Unit ships a Klausur-style exam example.

[**oral exam ("Kommunikationsprüfung").**]{#kommunikationsprufung}
The oral component of the school-leaving examination ("Abitur") in
Englisch — a monologue + dialogue task with stimulus material.
Track E Kl. 11–13 includes oral exam ("Kommunikationsprüfung")-style
Units in addition to schriftliche school-leaving examination
("Abitur") examples.

[**competency expectation (Kompetenzerwartung).**]{#kompetenzerwartung}
The curriculum framework ("Bildungsplan")'s statement of what
learners should be able to do at the end of a given year group
("Klassenstufe") or Niveau. Every Unit lists the specific
Kompetenzen it covers.

[**grading scale (Notenschlüssel).**]{#notenschlussel} The
standard BW grade-conversion scale from points to Note 1-6.

[**secondary school certificate ("Mittlerer Bildungsabschluss") /
intermediate secondary certificate ("Realschulabschluss").**]{#mittlerer-bildungsabschluss}
The qualification at the end of Klasse 10 in Track G+M.

[**school-leaving examination ("Abitur").**]{#abitur} The university-
entrance qualification at the end of Klasse 13 in Track E.
Includes a schriftliche Prüfung (Comprehension + Analysis +
Composition + Mediation, 90 BE) and an oral exam
("Kommunikationsprüfung").

[**Niveau G / M / E.**]{#niveau-g-m-e} The three Niveaustufen of
the comprehensive school ("Gesamtschule"): *grundlegend* (G, basic
secondary school certificate (Hauptschulabschluss)-bound),
*mittlere* (M, intermediate secondary certificate
("Realschulabschluss")-bound), and *erweitert* (E, school-leaving
examination ("Abitur")-bound). This site treats G+M as one track
running Kl. 5–10 and E as a separate track running Kl. 5–13.

[**language mediation (Sprachmittlung) / mediation ("Mediation").**]{#sprachmittlung}
Conveying the gist of a text from one language into another for a
specific purpose and audience — not translation. mediation
("Mediation") is examined in Klassenarbeiten from Kl. 7 onwards and
is a mandatory school-leaving examination ("Abitur") task component.

[**Inhalt / Sprache.**]{#inhalt-sprache} The two axes BW exam
markers score along. Inhalt = content (thesis clarity, evidence,
engagement with source). Sprache = language (vocabulary range,
grammatical accuracy, register, idiomaticity, cohesion). Weighting:
50/50 in Basisfach (basic course); 40/60 in Leistungsfach
(advanced course).

[**BE (Bewertungseinheiten).**]{#be} Marking points in the BW
exam system. A typical Klausur is 90 BE; a Klassenarbeit is 30
(Sek I lower) to 60 (Sek I upper).
