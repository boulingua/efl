## Learning objectives

- *I can describe my morning, afternoon, and evening in detail.*
- *I can use the present simple with time expressions and sequence words.*
- *I can use object pronouns (me, him, her, us, them).*

## Lead-in story

Sam wakes up at 6:30. Brushes teeth. Lets the cat out. Eats two pieces of toast and three slices of cheese, in that exact order. Walks to school in less than twelve minutes. Lina wakes up at 7:00, runs out the door at 7:25, and arrives at school out of breath but before the bell. Sam still doesn't understand how. Lina says: *the bell is on my side*.

## 1. Activate {#activate}
**Routine line-up.** Class lines up by *time of waking* without speaking — just signal numbers. Then by *length of breakfast*. Then by *minutes from door to school*. Three quick rounds.

## 2. Input {#input}
### Vocabulary — daily routines (richer)

*get up, wake up, brush my teeth, get dressed, have a shower, have breakfast, leave home, take the bus, start school, have a break, have lunch, finish school, do homework, have dinner, watch TV, read, go to bed.*

### Grammar — present simple + sequence words

Sequence: *first, then, after that, before, afterwards, finally.*
- *First I get up. Then I have breakfast.*
- *After that I take the bus.*
- *Before bed I read for ten minutes.*

### Grammar — object pronouns

| Subject | Object |
|---------|--------|
| I | me |
| you | you |
| he | him |
| she | her |
| it | it |
| we | us |
| they | them |

- *Sam helps me. I see her at the bus stop. Can you give him the book?*

## 3. Practise {#practise}
### Niveau E — controlled

1. Subject → object: I → ___ ; he → ___ ; we → ___ ; they → ___ .
2. Sequence fill-in: ___ I get up. ___ I have breakfast. ___ I leave home.

### Niveau E — productive

3. Build 6 sentences about your morning routine using 5 different sequence words.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**Controlled.** 1. me / him / us / them.
2. *First / Then / Finally* (or similar).

**Productive.** 3. Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce {#apply}
**Pair speaking — *Routine Swap (Niveau E)*.** 90 sec each direction. Cover morning, school, evening + one sequence-word phrase per turn. Report back to a different pair using object pronouns.

### Sample

*— First I get up at 6:30. Then I have breakfast with my brother — I help him with his maths. After that we walk to school together.*

## 5. Reflect {#reflect}
- [ ] I can describe my routine using 5 sequence words.
- [ ] I can use object pronouns correctly.
- [ ] I can report a partner's routine using *him/her*.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence using something you learned in this Unit.*

## Common pitfalls

- *I get up to seven* → ✗ / *I get up at seven* → ✓.
- *Sam helps I* → ✗ / *Sam helps me* → ✓.
- Sequence words don't replace tense logic.
