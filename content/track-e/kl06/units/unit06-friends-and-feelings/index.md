## Learning objectives

- *I can name 14 feelings (basic + nuanced).*
- *I can ask *How are you?* in 6 different ways.*
- *I can use *because*, *so*, and *although* in short reasons.*

## Lead-in story

Lina is in a strange mood. Sam asks why. "I don't know," she says. "I'm tired and slightly proud and a tiny bit jealous all at the same time." Sam nods. "That's called Tuesday afternoon," he says. "It happens to everyone."

## 1. Activate {#activate}
**Mood scan.** Stand on a line: *very happy* (left wall) → *very sad* (right wall). Find your spot. No talking. Teacher reads three short statements; students adjust position.

## 2. Input {#input}
### Vocabulary — feelings (richer)

*happy, sad, tired, excited, proud, nervous, scared, angry, jealous, calm, surprised, disappointed, embarrassed, grateful, frustrated, content, overwhelmed, focused.*

### Grammar — *because / so / although*

**because** + reason:
- *I'm tired **because** I went to bed late.*

**so** + result:
- *I went to bed late, **so** I'm tired.*

**although** + contrast:
- ***Although** I went to bed late, I am not tired.*

### Asking *How are you?*

- *How are you?* / *How are you doing?* / *How's it going?* / *How was your day?* / *Are you OK?* / *How are things?*

## 3. Practise {#practise}
### Niveau E — controlled

1. Match: happy — :), sad — :(, surprised — :O, tired — zzz.
2. Fill in *because*, *so*, *although*: I'm hungry __________ I didn't eat. He's tired, __________ he is going to bed. ___ I am tired, I am happy.

### Niveau E — productive

3. Build 6 sentences with *because*, *so*, and *although* (two each) about your real day.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**Controlled.** 1. all true.
2. because / so / Although.

**Productive.** 3. Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce {#apply}
**Pair speaking — *Today's Mood (Niveau E)*.** 2 min each. Use 4 feelings + 1 *because* + 1 *so* + 1 *although*.

### Sample

*— I'm a bit nervous because we have a maths test.*
*— Although I'm tired, I am also proud — I worked hard last week, so today's test feels less scary.*

## 5. Reflect {#reflect}
- [ ] I can name 14 feelings.
- [ ] I can ask *How are you?* in 6 ways.
- [ ] I can use *because*, *so*, *although* in reasons.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence using something you learned in this Unit.*

## Common pitfalls

- *because + clause*; *because of + noun*: *because the rain* → ✗ / *because of the rain* / *because it was raining* → ✓.
- *although + clause*: *Although tired …* → informal, but in writing prefer *Although I am tired*.
- Pastoral note: keep prompts low-stakes.
