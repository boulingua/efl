### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "My name is Sam. First I get up at 6:30. Then I have breakfast at 7. Afterwards I leave home at 7:30. School starts at 8. I finish at 1:20."

1. Get up: ___ . 2. Breakfast: ___ . 3. Leave home: ___ . 4. Finish school: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read.

> "On Sundays Lina wakes up late. First she has a long breakfast with her family. After that she meets Sam in the park. They play chess for an hour. Finally, in the evening, she does her homework."

1. Wake up: ___ . 2. Breakfast: ___ . 3. Park: ___ . 4. Evening: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Object pronoun.**

1. I see ___ every day. (Lina = ?)
2. He helps ___ with maths. (you and me = ?)
3. They invite ___ to the party. (you and Lina = ?)
4. We meet ___ at the bus stop. (Sam and Lina = ?)

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write 6 sentences about your typical Saturday routine using 4 sequence words.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** 6:30, 7:00, 7:30, 1:20.
**T2.** late, with her family, with Sam, homework.
**T3.** her / us / you / them.
**T4.** Open.
{{< /callout >}}
