## Lead-in story

Lina spent a week at the seaside in Cornwall. The sea was cold even in August. Her cousin caught one tiny crab and released it three minutes later, looking slightly betrayed. Sam stayed home and finally cleaned his desk. The desk was deeply impressed. Both of them claim to have had the better holiday, with no clear winner.

## 1. Activate

**Map prompt.** Sticky notes on a blank world map: *one place I have been, one I want to visit, one I never want to visit.* Compare with another pair.

## 2. Input

### Vocabulary — holidays

*seaside, beach, cliffs, mountains, countryside, city break, campsite, hotel, hostel, suitcase, passport, souvenir, postcard, sunscreen, life jacket, lighthouse, harbour, ferry.*

### Grammar — past simple (positive / negative / Q)

**Regular** + *-ed*: *worked, played, watched.*
**Irregular** changes: *go → went, see → saw, eat → ate, swim → swam, take → took, have → had, find → found, fly → flew, leave → left, meet → met.*

Negatives & questions: *did + base verb*.
- *Did you go? / I didn't go.*
- *Did she eat? / She didn't eat.*

## 3. Practise

### Niveau E — controlled

1. Past simple: I __________ (visit) my grandmother. We __________ (go) to the sea. We __________ (eat) fish. They __________ (find) a small crab.
2. Make negative + question: *We saw a crab* → ___ ; *They went* → ___ .

### Niveau E — productive

3. Build 6 past-simple sentences about a real or imagined holiday: place, weather, transport, food, one fun thing, one feeling.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**Controlled.** 1. visited / went / ate / found.
2. *We didn't see a crab. / Did we see a crab? They didn't go. / Did they go?*

**Productive.** 3. Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce

**Pair speaking — *Holiday Interview (Niveau E)*.** 90 seconds each direction. Six questions minimum: where, when, who with, weather, transport, best moment, one thing that didn't work.

### Sample

*— Where did you go?* — *I went to Cornwall with my family.* — *Did anything not go to plan?* — *Yes — we missed the ferry on the second day.*

## 5. Reflect

- [ ] I can use past simple in pos/neg/Q.
- [ ] I can describe a place with 6+ adjectives.
- [ ] I can hold a 90-second holiday interview.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence using something you learned in this Unit.*

## Exam example

{{< callout type="warning" title="class test (\"Klassenarbeit\") — Niveau E (45 minutes)" icon="false" >}}
**Time.** 45 minutes. **Total.** 30 points.
{{< /callout >}}

### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "Last summer Lina went to Cornwall with her family. They stayed in a small hotel near the harbour. The sea was cold but the sun was warm. They saw seals from the cliffs. They ate fish and chips on the beach."

1. Where: ___ . 2. Stay: ___ . 3. Animal: ___ . 4. Food: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read.

> "Sam stayed at home last summer. He cleaned his desk, read three books, learned how to bake bread, and started a small notebook of recipes. He didn't travel, but he says it was the calmest summer of his life."

1. Did Sam travel? ___ . 2. Three things he did: ___ . 3. New skill: ___ . 4. Description: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Past simple.**

1. We __________ (visit) Cornwall.
2. They __________ (not / go) to Spain.
3. ___ you ___ (see) the sea?
4. He __________ (eat) fish and chips.
5. ___ she ___ (find) the right station?

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write 6 sentences about a real or imagined holiday.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** Cornwall, small hotel near harbour, seals, fish and chips.
**T2.** No, cleaned desk / read three books / learned to bake bread, baking bread, the calmest of his life.
**T3.** 1. visited, 2. didn't go, 3. Did … see, 4. ate, 5. Did … find.
**T4.** Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## Common pitfalls

- *I goed* → ✗ / *I went* → ✓ (irregular).
- *Did you went?* → ✗ / *Did you go?* → ✓ (after *did*, base form).
- Past time marker + present perfect = ✗.
