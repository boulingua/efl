### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "This is the announcement for the 09:14 to Karlsruhe. The train will leave from Platform 5. Please mind the gap between the train and the platform. The next train to Frankfurt is delayed by ten minutes."

1. To Karlsruhe — time: ___ . 2. Platform: ___ . 3. Watch out for: ___ . 4. Frankfurt: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read the timetable above. Answer.

1. Platform for Karlsruhe: ___ .
2. Departure to Strasbourg: ___ .
3. Arrival in Paris: ___ .
4. Total Stuttgart → Paris: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Fill in train vocabulary.**

1. A __________ to London, please. (one-way)
2. The train from __________ 5.
3. The train is __________ by ten minutes.
4. The next train __________ at 09:14.

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write an 8-line ticket-office dialogue.
