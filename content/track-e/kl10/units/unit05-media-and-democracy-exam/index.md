### Task 1 — Listening (10 BE)

Listen twice.

> "More than 200 local UK papers have closed. The evidence suggests this is linked to lower voter turnout and less scrutiny of councils. The link is not necessarily causal — affected districts also tend to be under economic stress. The link is, however, consistent across studies."

### Task 2 — Reading (12 BE)

Read the *Local Papers, Local Voters* extract above.

1. Three linked effects: ___ . 2. Caveat about causality: ___ . 3. Replacement examples: ___ . 4. Whether replacements work: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (10 BE)

**Insert academic hedge.**

2. ___ that the link is causal.
3. ___ , citizen journalism has filled the gap.
4. ___ ; the variation across districts is real.

### Task 4 — Writing (13 BE)

Write 220 words: an op-ed on a media-democracy topic. Use 4 academic hedges.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** 200+; lower voter turnout / less scrutiny of councils; districts also under economic stress; consistent across studies.
**T2.** lower voter turnout / less scrutiny of councils / slower response on local environment; districts often under broader economic stress; hyper-local newsletters and citizen journalism; in some contexts yes, in others no.
