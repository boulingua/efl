### Task 1 — Listening (10 BE)

Listen twice.

> "You should keep a record of every purchase. You shouldn't lend more than you can afford to lose. You'd better start small — three months of pocket money is enough to learn the habit."

1. Should keep: ___ . 2. Shouldn't lend: ___ . 3. Had better: ___ . 4. Length: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (12 BE)

Read.

> "Eli started a small green notebook. By month four he could see the shape of his spending: half on food outside the home, a quarter on small things he didn't remember buying. He decided to cut the small things."

1. Notebook colour: ___ . 2. Half spending: ___ . 3. Quarter spending: ___ . 4. Decision: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (10 BE)

**Fill in *should / shouldn't / had better*.**

1. You __________ save 10 %.
2. You __________ lend what you can't afford.
3. You __________ start now, before prices change.
4. We __________ keep all receipts.

### Task 4 — Writing (13 BE)

Write 120 words about a money decision. Use *should / shouldn't / had better* (3 in total).

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** every purchase, more than you can afford to lose, start small, three months.
**T2.** small green; half — food outside the home; quarter — small things not remembered; cut the small things.
**T3.** should / shouldn't / had better / should.
**T4.** Open.
