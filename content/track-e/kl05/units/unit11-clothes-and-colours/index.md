## Learning objectives

- *I can name 12 items of clothing and 10 colours.*
- *I can describe what someone is wearing using present continuous.*
- *I can use comparative *the same … as / different from*.*

## Lead-in story

Theo's blue jumper has gone missing. He looks under the bed, in the wardrobe, behind the door. Frida the fox steps quietly out of the wardrobe wearing a small blue jumper that fits her remarkably well. "Foxes," she says, "appreciate clean wool." Theo opens his mouth to protest, then closes it. He has been raised well.

## 1. Activate

**Colour shout.** Teacher points; class shouts the colour. 30 seconds, fast pace.

## 2. Input

### Vocabulary — clothes and colours

*Clothes:* T-shirt, shirt, jumper, jacket, coat, trousers, jeans, shorts, skirt, dress, shoes, trainers, boots, socks, hat, scarf, gloves.
*Colours:* red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, grey, brown, orange, pink, purple, navy, beige.

### Grammar — present continuous for outfits

- *Mia is wearing a yellow jumper and blue jeans.*
- *I am wearing a black T-shirt today.*
- *Theo is not wearing a coat.*
- *Are you wearing your new shoes?*

### Comparative phrases

- *the same colour as*: *My T-shirt is the same colour as your scarf.*
- *different from*: *My shoes are different from yours.*
- *bigger / smaller than*: *His shoes are bigger than mine.*

## 3. Practise

### Niveau E — controlled

1. Build present continuous: I __________ (wear) blue jeans. Mia __________ (wear) a green jacket.
2. Compare: my hat / your scarf — same colour → ___ ; his shoes / mine — bigger → ___ .

### Niveau E — productive

3. Describe a partner in 4 sentences. Add one comparison.
4. Negative + question: *I'm not wearing …*; *Are you wearing …?* — three each.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**Controlled.** 1. am wearing / is wearing.
2. *My hat is the same colour as your scarf. His shoes are bigger than mine.*

**Productive.** 3-4. Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce

**Description game.** In pairs, A closes their eyes; B describes a third classmate's outfit (4 sentences minimum, one comparison). A guesses who.

### Sample

*— She is wearing a red T-shirt and black jeans. Her shoes are white. Her scarf is the same colour as her T-shirt.*
*— Lina!*

## 5. Reflect

- [ ] I can name 12 clothes and 10 colours.
- [ ] I can use present continuous for outfits.
- [ ] I can compare two items with *same … as*, *different from*.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence that uses
something you learned in this Unit.*

## Exam example

{{< callout type="warning" title="class test (\"Klassenarbeit\") — Niveau E (45 minutes)" icon="false" >}}
**Time.** 45 minutes. **Total.** 30 points.
{{< /callout >}}

### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "Mia is wearing a yellow jumper, blue jeans, and white trainers. Theo is wearing a green T-shirt, black shorts, and brown sandals. Lina is wearing the same colour scarf as Mia's jumper."

1. Mia jumper: ___ . 2. Mia shoes: ___ . 3. Theo T-shirt: ___ . 4. Lina's scarf colour: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read.

> "On the school trip we must wear comfortable shoes, a warm jumper, and a rain jacket. No skirts, no flip-flops. Pack one extra pair of socks in your rucksack."

1. Shoes: ___ . 2. Jumper: ___ . 3. Bring: ___ . 4. Don't wear: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Build the present continuous.**

1. (I / wear / blue / jeans) → ___
2. (Mia / wear / red / dress) → ___
3. (we / wear / school / uniform) → ___
4. (Theo / not / wear / hat) → ___

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Describe what three classmates are wearing today. Use present continuous and one comparison.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** yellow, white trainers, green, yellow.
**T2.** comfortable, warm, rain jacket / extra socks, skirts / flip-flops.
**T3.** 1. *I am wearing blue jeans.* 2. *Mia is wearing a red dress.* 3. *We are wearing school uniform.* 4. *Theo isn't wearing a hat.*
**T4.** Open.
{{< /callout >}}

## Common pitfalls

- *She wear a red dress* → ✗ / *She is wearing* → ✓.
- *pant* (US, underwear) vs. *trousers* (BrE, outerwear).
- L1 trap: German *Hose* (singular) is English *trousers* (plural).
