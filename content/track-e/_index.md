**Track E** runs from **Klasse 5 to Klasse 13** — nine school years
at the **erweitertes Niveau (E)**, finishing with the
*Allgemeine Hochschulreife (Abitur)*. Each Klasse ships **12 Units**;
Klassen 11–13 are organised as Basisfach (BF) and Leistungsfach (LF).

Track E shares its theme arc with [Track G+M](../track-gm/) for
Klassen 5–10 so mixed-Niveau classrooms hold to a common subject;
from Klasse 11 onwards Track E becomes an exam-grade Oberstufe
curriculum keyed to the *Bildungsplan Sekundarstufe II*.

## Klassen on this track

- [**Klasse 6** — Adventures and routines](kl06/) — daily life, holidays, friendship, simple stories.
- [**Klasse 7** — Identity and the wider world](kl07/) — growing up, media, intercultural lens.
- [**Klasse 8** — Belonging and fairness](kl08/) — diversity, the city, civic English.
- [**Klasse 9** — Choices and society](kl09/) — work, environment, the future.
- [**Klasse 10** — Transition and the world](kl10/) — bridge year toward Oberstufe.
- [**Klasse 11** — Cultural entry, literary voice](kl11/) — first set texts (Ishiguro, Shakespeare), Basisfach + Leistungsfach.
- [**Klasse 12** — Discourse and analysis](kl12/) — dystopia, post-colonial voices, Klausur composition.
- [**Klasse 13** — Exam-grade and issue-framed](kl13/) — Abitur preparation: globalisation, identity, mediation.
