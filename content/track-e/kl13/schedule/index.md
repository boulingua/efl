The twelve Units of Grade 13 (E) across the school
year. Each Unit covers roughly three teaching weeks.

| Unit | Title | Skills focus | Exam type |
|------|-------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | [Globalisation and the Self](/track-e/kl13/units/unit01-globalisation-and-the-self/) | reading, writing, intercultural | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 2 | [Political Discourse, Advanced](/track-e/kl13/units/unit02-political-discourse-advanced/) | reading, listening, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 3 | [Dystopias and Utopias](/track-e/kl13/units/unit03-dystopias-and-utopias/) | reading, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 4 | [Contemporary Poetry](/track-e/kl13/units/unit04-contemporary-poetry/) | reading, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 5 | [Media and Public Opinion](/track-e/kl13/units/unit05-media-and-public-opinion/) | reading, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 6 | [Mediation: A German Policy Text](/track-e/kl13/units/unit06-mediation-policy-text/) | mediation, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 7 | [The Abitur Essay](/track-e/kl13/units/unit07-the-abitur-essay/) | writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 8 | [The Abitur Comprehension Task](/track-e/kl13/units/unit08-the-abitur-comprehension/) | reading, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 9 | [The Abitur Analysis Task](/track-e/kl13/units/unit09-the-abitur-analysis/) | reading, writing, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 10 | [Kommunikationsprüfung: Full Mock](/track-e/kl13/units/unit10-kommunikationspruefung-full-mock/) | speaking, listening, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 11 | [Issue-Framed Debate](/track-e/kl13/units/unit11-issue-framed-debate/) | speaking, listening, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
| 12 | [Year Review and Handover](/track-e/kl13/units/unit12-year-review-and-handover/) | writing, speaking, language_awareness | Abitur-grade Klausur (school-leaving examination) |
