This is the cross-Unit material library. Every Unit on the site ships
two attached files:

- a **slide deck** (`.pptx`) — the same kind of one-source slide that
  drives the in-class lesson, exported for re-mixing or printing as
  handouts.
- a **worksheet** (`.pdf`) — the single-page A4 worksheet that goes with
  each Unit, suitable for self-study or for a substitute teacher.

Both are placeholders during the migration; real materials drop in at
the same canonical paths and the hub picks them up automatically.

## Browse

- **[Presentations](presentations/)** — 180 slide decks across all
  fifteen courses, two tracks, Klassen 5 to 13.
- **[Worksheets](worksheets/)** — 180 worksheets, one per Unit.

Each listing supports tag-based filtering (track, Klassenstufe, Niveau)
and full-text search on title.
