A two-track English curriculum for comprehensive school —
G+M from Grade 5 to 10, and E from Grade 5 through the school-leaving
examination ("Abitur") in Grade 13. Aligned to a regional curriculum
framework for Sekundarstufe I and to the curriculum framework for
the gymnasiale Oberstufe (see [References](/references/)).
Authored by S. Le Boulanger.
{{< /lead >}}
{{< /hero >}}

## What this is

Fifteen courses, one per year group per track, each with twelve Units.
Every Unit ships as both an HTML article and a Reveal.js slide deck,
written from a single Quarto source. Every Unit closes with a worked
exam example — a class test ("Klassenarbeit") at the appropriate Niveau
in Grades 5–10, and an Abitur-style Prüfungsaufgabe in Grades 11–13.
The site is authored, not aggregated: every text, transcript, exercise,
and exam stimulus is original to S. Le Boulanger.

## Who this is for

Teachers on either track at a comprehensive school who want a
ready-to-teach curriculum-aligned skeleton — both for grundlegend
and mittleres Niveau (G+M, leading to Hauptschul- and intermediate
secondary certificate) and for the erweitertes Niveau heading toward
Abitur (Basisfach and Leistungsfach). The materials are designed to
be used as-is in class, adapted for differentiated groups, or remixed
under CC-BY 4.0.

## The five-step template

Every Unit walks the same path: **Activate → Input → Practise →
Produce → Reflect**. Exam-preparation Units use the variant **Task →
Model → Strategy → Attempt → Feedback**. The shared shape lets
learners predict what comes next while teachers can drop in
differentiated material at any step.

## One source, two formats

A single `.qmd` file becomes the Unit's HTML article *and* the slide
deck. The article is for self-study, homework catch-up, and parent
read-along. The slide deck drives the lesson itself, with speaker
notes that cover timing, transitions, and Niveau-aware
differentiation in mixed classrooms.

## Exam preparation

Every Unit closes with a worked exam example at the right Niveau.
Grades 5–10 end with a kompetenzorientierte class test ("Klassenarbeit").
Grades 11–13 end with an Abitur-style task — schriftliche Prüfung
(Comprehension + Analysis + Composition + Mediation) or
Kommunikationsprüfung — labelled Basisfach or Leistungsfach.
