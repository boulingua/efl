### Task 1 — Listening (10 BE)

Listen twice.

> "Die Stadtverwaltung hat angekündigt, dass die Linie 42 ab dem 1. September einen neuen Fahrplan haben wird. Die Fahrtzeit verlängert sich um etwa fünf Minuten."

1. Topic: ___ . 2. Date: ___ . 3. Time change: ___ . 4. Source-language: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (12 BE)

Read the German source above. Answer in English.

1. Authority: ___ . 2. Date of change: ___ . 3. Two extra stops in: ___ . 4. Action requested: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (10 BE)

**Reporting-verb fill-in.**

1. The article __________ that line 42 has a new timetable. (says)
2. Officials __________ passengers to check online. (advise)
3. The author __________ that the change is small. (claims)
4. Critics __________ that elderly passengers will be affected. (warn)

### Task 4 — Writing (13 BE)

Mediate: write 6 English sentences from the German source for a friend abroad. Use 3 reporting verbs.

**T2.** city council; 1 September; the eastern part of town; check the online timetable.
**T3.** says / advise / claims / warn.
**T4.** Open.
