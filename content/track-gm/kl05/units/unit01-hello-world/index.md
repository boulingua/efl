## Learning objectives

- *I can say my name, age, and town in English.*
- *I can ask three questions to introduce myself to a new person.*
- *I can use the verb *to be* (am / is / are) correctly in short
  sentences.*

## Bildungsplan alignment

- **3.1.1** — daily life, school, family.
- **3.1.3.3** — short introductory exchange.
- **3.1.3.5** — short personal text.
- **3.1.3.8** — *to be* in short sentences.

(Source: <https://www.bildungsplaene-bw.de/,Lde/LS/BP2016BW/ALLG/SEK1/E1>)

## Lead-in story

Mia stands at the door of Klasse 5b. The room is new. The chairs
are new. Theo, her brother, sits in the back row. He waves. A small
red fox is sitting on the windowsill. The fox is *not* normally
there. Mia rubs her eyes. The fox waves. Mia waves back. "Hello,"
the fox says. "I'm Frida. Welcome to English."

## 1. Activate

**Stand up — sit down.**

The teacher says a sentence. If it is true for you, stand up. If
it is not, stay seated.

- *I am ten years old.*
- *I live in Stuttgart.*
- *I have one brother.*

## 2. Input

### Hello chant

> Hello, hello, what's your name?\
> My name is Mia, what's your name?\
> I'm Theo, nice to meet you!\
> Nice to meet you too!

### Vocabulary — meeting people

| English | Deutsch |
|---------|---------|
| Hello / Hi | Hallo |
| Good morning | Guten Morgen |
| Goodbye / Bye | Tschüss |
| Please | Bitte |
| Thank you | Danke |
| Nice to meet you | Schön, dich kennenzulernen |
| What's your name? | Wie heißt du? |
| How old are you? | Wie alt bist du? |
| Where do you live? | Wo wohnst du? |

### Grammar — *to be*

| Person | Form |
|--------|------|
| I | am |
| you | are |
| he / she / it | is |
| we / you / they | are |

- *I **am** Mia. I **am** ten.*
- *Theo **is** my brother.*
- *We **are** in Klasse 5.*

## 3. Practise

### Niveau G

**A. Fill in *am, is, are*.**

1. I __________ Mia. 2. Theo __________ my brother.
3. We __________ friends. 4. Frida __________ a fox.

### Niveau M

**B. Build the question.**

5. (your name?) → ___ 6. (your age?) → ___
7. (your town?) → ___

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**G.** 1. am, 2. is, 3. are, 4. is.\
**M.** 5. What's your name? 6. How old are you? 7. Where do you
live?
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce

**Mingling task.** Walk around the classroom. Find three new
people. Ask name, age, town. Write each answer in your notebook.

### Sample exchange

> "Hello, what's your name?"\
> "I'm Lina. What's yours?"\
> "I'm Theo. How old are you, Lina?"\
> "I'm ten. And you?"\
> "I'm eleven. Where do you live?"

## 5. Reflect

- [ ] I can say my name, age, town.
- [ ] I can ask three introducing questions.
- [ ] I can use *am, is, are*.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Write one sentence about a new
person you met today.*

## Exam example

{{< callout type="warning" title="class test (\"Klassenarbeit\") — Niveau M (45 minutes)" icon="false" >}}

**Time.** 45 minutes.\
**Total.** 30 points.

### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

The teacher reads aloud. Tick the right answer.

> "Hello! My name is Mia. I am ten years old. I live in Stuttgart.
> I have one brother. His name is Theo."

1. Her name is __________ . (Mia / Tia)
2. She is __________ years old. (nine / ten)
3. She lives in __________ . (Stuttgart / Frankfurt)
4. Her brother is __________ . (Theo / Tim)

### Task 2 — Reading (10 BE)

Read the short text and complete the table.

> "Hi, I'm Theo. I am eleven. My favourite colour is blue. I love
> football. My best friend is Mia."

| Name | Age | Colour | Hobby | Friend |
|------|-----|--------|-------|--------|
| Theo |  ?  |   ?    |   ?   |   ?    |

### Task 3 — Use of English (6 BE)

Fill in the right form of *to be*.

1. I __________ ten.
2. She __________ my friend.
3. We __________ in Klasse 5.

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write 4 sentences about yourself: name, age, town, one thing you
like.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
1. Mia, 2. ten, 3. Stuttgart, 4. Theo.\
**Task 2.** Theo / 11 / blue / football / Mia.\
**Task 3.** am, is, are.\
**Task 4.** Open. 1 BE per correct sentence + 2 BE language.

## Common pitfalls

- *I is ten* → ✗ / *I am ten* → ✓.
- *How you are?* → ✗ / *How are you?* → ✓.
- L1 trap: German *Wie heißt du?* literally maps to *What are you
  called?* — the standard English is *What's your name?*
