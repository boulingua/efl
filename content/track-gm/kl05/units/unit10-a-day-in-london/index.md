## Lead-in story

Mia's class is in London on a school trip. Big Ben is louder than she expected. The pigeons are bigger. The buses are red and have two floors. Theo wants to ride on the top of every bus they see. Mr. Flint says: "We have a list. We follow the list."

## 1. Activate

**London quiz — five guesses.** With your partner, name: a river, a tower, a famous palace, a museum, a famous square.

## 2. Input

### Vocabulary — London landmarks

*Big Ben, the Tower of London, Tower Bridge, Buckingham Palace, Trafalgar Square, the British Museum, the London Eye, the Thames.*

### Useful phrases — directions

- *Excuse me, where is …?*
- *Go straight on.*
- *Turn left / right.*
- *It's on your left / right.*
- *Take the second street on the right.*

### Reading — A class trip

We arrived at King's Cross at 10 a.m. The first stop was the British Museum. Theo wanted to see the mummies. Mia wanted to see the clocks. We had lunch in a small park. After lunch we walked across Tower Bridge. The river was grey, but the bridge was bright blue. At 5 p.m. we took the train back.

## 3. Practise

### Niveau G

1. Match: Big Ben — clock, Buckingham Palace — queen, Tower Bridge — river. (T / F)
2. Excuse me, where __________ the museum? (be)
3. Go __________ on. (right / straight)
4. Turn __________ at the corner. (right / on)

### Niveau M

5. (you / give / direction to bus stop / two streets / right) → ___
6. (you / ask / the way to Big Ben) → ___

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**G.** 1. all true.
2. is.
3. straight.
4. right.

**M.** 5. *Go straight on. Take the second street on the right. The bus stop is on your left.*
6. *Excuse me, how do I get to Big Ben?*
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce

**London-trip plan.** In groups of 3, plan a 6-hour London day. Choose 4 places. Write a small itinerary (time, place, activity).

### Sample

*10:00 — British Museum (mummies). 11:30 — walk to Trafalgar Square. 12:30 — lunch. 14:00 — London Eye. 16:00 — train back.*

## Exam example

{{< callout type="warning" title="class test (\"Klassenarbeit\") — Niveau M (45 minutes)" icon="false" >}}
**Time.** 45 minutes. **Total.** 30 points.
{{< /callout >}}

### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "Excuse me, where is Trafalgar Square? Go straight on for two minutes. Then turn left. The square is on your right."

1. The visitor asks about ___ .
2. First, go ___ .
3. Then turn ___ .
4. The square is on the ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read about Mia's trip.

> "In London I saw Big Ben, the London Eye, and Tower Bridge. My favourite was the London Eye, because the view was amazing."

1. Three places: ___ . 2. Favourite: ___ . 3. Why: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Choose: *in / on / at*.**

1. ___ London, 2. ___ the bridge, 3. ___ 5 p.m., 4. ___ the museum (= inside).

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write 4–6 sentences about a city trip you would like to take. Include 3 places.

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** Trafalgar Square, straight on, left, right.
**T2.** Big Ben / London Eye / Tower Bridge; London Eye; the view was amazing.
**T3.** 1. in, 2. on, 3. at, 4. in.
**T4.** Open.

## Common pitfalls

- *Big Ben is a tower / clock* — actually a bell; the tower is the *Elizabeth Tower*.
- Stereotype check: avoid *all British people drink tea*.
- L1 trap: German *Wo ist der Bahnhof?* maps to *Where is the station?* — no extra *do*.
