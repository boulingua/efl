## Learning objectives

- *I can name family members in English.*
- *I can use the possessive 's to talk about belongings and
  relationships.*
- *I can write a short text (4–6 sentences) about my family.*

## Bildungsplan alignment

- **3.1.1** — family life.
- **3.1.3.5** — short personal text.
- **3.1.3.7** — family-vocabulary cluster.
- **3.1.3.8** — possessive 's.

## Lead-in story

Mia draws her family on the kitchen table. She draws her mother
with a stethoscope. Her father with a laptop. Her brother Theo
with a football. Pepper the cat sleeps on the corner of the
paper. Frida the fox looks at the drawing through the window. "Am
I in the family?" Frida asks. Mia thinks for a moment. Then she
draws a small fox in the corner of the page.

## 1. Activate {#activate}
**Family tree quick-draw.** In your notebook, draw three boxes.
Write a name in each box. One person from your family. Tell your
partner who they are.

## 2. Input {#input}
### Vocabulary — family

| English | Deutsch |
|---------|---------|
| mother / mum | Mutter / Mama |
| father / dad | Vater / Papa |
| sister | Schwester |
| brother | Bruder |
| grandmother / grandma | Oma |
| grandfather / grandpa | Opa |
| aunt | Tante |
| uncle | Onkel |
| cousin | Cousin / Cousine |
| parents | Eltern |

### Grammar — possessive 's

We add **'s** to a person to show belonging or relationship.

- *Mia's brother* = the brother of Mia.
- *Theo's bag* = the bag of Theo.
- *the cat's name* = the name of the cat.

For plural nouns ending in *-s*, just add an apostrophe:

- *my parents' car* = the car of my parents.

## 3. Practise {#practise}
### Niveau G

**A. Match.** 1. mother — a. Schwester. 2. brother — b. Onkel.
3. uncle — c. Mutter. 4. sister — d. Bruder.

### Niveau M

**B. Possessive 's.**

1. (Mia / brother) → ___ 2. (Theo / bag) → ___
3. (the cat / name) → ___ 4. (my friends / school) → ___

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**G.** 1–c, 2–d, 3–b, 4–a.\
**M.** 1. Mia's brother. 2. Theo's bag. 3. The cat's name.
4. My friends' school.
{{< /callout >}}

## 4. Produce {#apply}
**Family-tree poster.** On A4 paper, draw and label four family
members. Underneath, write four sentences using *to be* and the
possessive 's.

### Sample (Niveau M)

> *This is my family. My mother's name is Petra. She is a
> teacher. My brother's name is Theo. He is eleven. We have a
> cat. The cat's name is Pepper.*

## 5. Reflect {#reflect}
- [ ] I can name eight family words.
- [ ] I can use the possessive 's correctly.
- [ ] I can write four sentences about my family.

**One thing in your notebook:** *Who is the youngest person in
your family?*

## Common pitfalls

- *the brother of Mia* → grammatically OK but unidiomatic. Prefer
  *Mia's brother*.
- Apostrophe placement: *its* (possessive, no apostrophe) vs.
  *it's* (= it is).
- L1 trap: German *Geschwister* has no single English word — use
  *brother(s) and sister(s)* or *siblings*.
