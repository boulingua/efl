**Track G+M** runs from **Klasse 5 to Klasse 10** — six school years at
the **grundlegendes (G)** and **mittleres (M)** Niveau. Each Klasse
ships **12 Units**, every Unit with an HTML article, a slide deck, a
worksheet, and an exam example
(*class test (`Klassenarbeit`)*).

The end-of-grade-10 destination is the *Mittlerer Bildungsabschluss*.
Track G+M shares its theme arc with [Track E](../track-e/) for
Klassen 5–10 so mixed-Niveau classrooms can hold to a common subject
even when their exam targets differ.

## Klassen on this track

Click into a Klasse to see its 12 Units, the year-long
[schedule](../track-gm/kl05/schedule/), the curriculum-aligned
*Kompetenzerwartungen*, and per-Unit downloads.

- [**Klasse 5** — Family, school, friends](kl05/) — concrete and personal: home, school, weather, food, animals, hobbies.
- [**Klasse 6** — Adventures and routines](kl06/) — daily life, holidays, friendship, simple stories.
- [**Klasse 7** — Identity and the wider world](kl07/) — growing up, media, mediation between languages.
- [**Klasse 8** — Belonging and fairness](kl08/) — diversity, the city, civic English.
- [**Klasse 9** — Choices and society](kl09/) — work, environment, the future.
- [**Klasse 10** — Transition, work, media](kl10/) — graduation year, world of work, longer texts.
