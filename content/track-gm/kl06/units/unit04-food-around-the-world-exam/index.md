**Time.** 45 minutes. **Total.** 30 points.

### Task 1 — Listening (8 BE)

Listen twice.

> "At our food day we tried sushi from Japan, samosa from India, falafel from the Middle East, and pierogi from Poland. The most popular dish was the falafel wrap."

1. Number of dishes: ___ . 2. From Japan: ___ . 3. From Poland: ___ . 4. Most popular: ___ .

### Task 2 — Reading (8 BE)

Read.

> "My favourite dish is Spätzle with cheese. My grandmother makes it on cold Sundays. The dough is thick and yellow. We eat it with crispy onions on top."

1. Dish: ___ . 2. Who makes it: ___ . 3. When: ___ . 4. Topping: ___ .

### Task 3 — Use of English (8 BE)

**Fill in the -ing form.**

1. I love __________ (cook).
2. She enjoys __________ (eat) sushi.
3. They like __________ (bake) bread.
4. We hate __________ (peel) onions.

### Task 4 — Writing (6 BE)

Write 5–6 sentences about a dish you love (country, ingredients, when, why).

{{< callout type="tip" title="Answer key" collapse="true" >}}
**T1.** 4, sushi, pierogi, falafel wrap.
**T2.** Spätzle with cheese, grandmother, cold Sundays, crispy onions.
**T3.** cooking / eating / baking / peeling.
**T4.** Open.
{{< /callout >}}
